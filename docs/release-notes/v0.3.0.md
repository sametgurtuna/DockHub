### What's New in v0.3.0

Quick audio output switching with an EarTrumpet-style mixer, connected device battery monitoring, a macOS-style Recycle Bin widget, and interaction polish.

---

#### Audio & Peripheral Management
- **Quick Audio Switcher & Volume Mixer:** Switch default audio output devices (headphones/speakers) with a single click or through the context menu. Scroll the mouse wheel over the widget to adjust master volume, or click to open an EarTrumpet-style per-application volume mixer flyout.
- **Connected Devices Battery Widget:** Monitor real-time battery levels for Bluetooth peripherals and 2.4 GHz RF wireless accessories (including HyperX Cloud II Wireless, Cloud Flight, and Cloud Alpha headsets) with color-coded percentages and charging status indicators.
- **macOS-Style Recycle Bin Widget:** Displays dynamic empty/full states, supports drag-and-drop file deletion directly onto the icon, and offers instant Recycle Bin emptying via right-click.
- **Hydration Tracker Widget:** Added a water drop icon and visual countdown timer to promote regular hydration.

---

#### Enhancements & Bug Fixes
- **Global Click-to-Dismiss:** Clicking anywhere outside open flyouts or menus smoothly dismisses active popups. Clicking a widget icon while its popover is active cleanly toggles it closed.
- **Audio Mixer Scroll Isolation:** Scrolling inside the volume mixer navigates the application list without unintentionally scrolling the dock or adjusting master volume.
- **HyperX Wireless Dongle Battery Reading:** Fixed an issue where 2.4 GHz USB dongles reported 0% battery by reading direct HID input reports (`col03`).
- **Windows 11 Audio Switching:** Fixed `REGDB_E_CLASSNOTREG` COM error during default audio endpoint switching.

---
