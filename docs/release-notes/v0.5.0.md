### What's New in v0.5.0

Introducing dock folders (stacks), fan-out spring wave animations, and organizational tools for decluttering your desktop.

---

#### Folders & Group Stacks
- **Organize Applications & Widgets:** Group related applications, development tools, games, or utilities into compact, expandable folders.
- **Dynamic 2x2 Grid Previews:** Collapsed folder icons display a clean 2x2 preview of their first four contained items.
- **Drag-to-Group:** Drag items directly onto an existing folder icon to add them instantly.

---

#### Staggered Spring Wave Animations
- **Fan-Out Expansion:** Clicking a folder triggers an arc or fan-out popover with fluid physics-based spring wave animation.
- **Orientation Awareness:** Folders adapt their expansion direction based on the dock position (bottom, top, left, right).
- **Custom Accents & Management:** Right-click folders to customize names, assign accent colors, reorder contents, or select *Ungroup all* to dissolve items back to the dock.

---

#### Enhancements & Fixes
- Added recursive item resolution in the configuration engine for nested folder items.
- Empty folders are automatically cleaned up when all items are removed.
- Seamless compatibility with both Dark and Light theme glass backdrops.

---
