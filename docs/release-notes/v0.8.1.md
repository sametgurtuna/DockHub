### What's New in v0.8.1

A targeted update introducing battery tracking for the LAMZU Atlantis Mini wireless mouse and an all-device management panel.

---

#### Hardware & Battery Features
- **LAMZU Atlantis Mini Support:** Direct 2.4 GHz RF receiver battery level reading without requiring vendor software to remain open.
- **Charging & Sleep States:** Accurate charging status when wired, with graceful retention of the last known battery level when entering power-saving sleep.
- **Device Management Panel:** Click the battery widget to view an overview of all connected devices with circular battery gauges, charging status, and custom ordering (▲ / ▼).
- **Persistent Priority:** Custom device display order is saved, keeping primary peripherals prominent on the dock.

---

#### Enhancements & Fixes
- Consolidated hardware device scanning into a single pass per refresh interval, lowering background USB bus polling.
- Added Turkish translations for the new battery management dialog.

---

### Download & Installation
- **Installer:** Download `DockHub-Setup-0.8.1-x64.exe` below. Existing installations can also update directly from *Settings › About*.
- **Requirements:** Windows 10 (1809+) or Windows 11 (64-bit).
