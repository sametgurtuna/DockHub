### ✨ What's New in v0.8.1

A small update to the **Device batteries** widget: it now reads the LAMZU Atlantis Mini, and a click opens a panel with all your devices, where you choose which one the dock shows.

#### 🖱️ LAMZU Atlantis Mini Support
- The widget now shows the battery level of the **LAMZU Atlantis Mini** through its 2.4 GHz receiver. You don't need to keep the LAMZU app open.
- DockHub only asks the receiver for the battery level. It never changes the mouse's settings (DPI, buttons, lighting).
- While the mouse is plugged in with its cable, it is shown as **charging**.
- If the mouse is asleep or switched off, the last known level stays on the dock instead of the device disappearing.

#### 🔋 All Your Devices in One Panel
- **Click the widget** (or its tile on a vertical dock) to open a panel listing every connected device: battery ring, name, percentage and charging state.
- **Reorder with the ▲ ▼ buttons.** The device at the top is the one the dock shows, marked **Shown on dock**. The *Multiple devices* layout shows the first three in the same order.
- Your order is saved. A device that is switched off keeps its place and returns to it when it connects again.
- The panel updates by itself while it's open, and it has a refresh button. You can also open it from the right-click menu with **Show all devices**.

### 🛠️ Improvements & Fixes
- Hardware battery scans look up connected USB devices once per refresh instead of once per device type.
- Turkish translations for the new panel.

### 📥 Download & Install
- **Installer:** Download `DockHub-Setup-0.8.1-x64.exe` from Assets below and run it. It upgrades your existing install in place and keeps your settings. If you have 0.8.0, you can also install it from **Settings > About**.
- **Requirements:** Windows 10 1809 or newer / Windows 11, x64.
