### What's New in v0.9.2

A stability and visual quality update addressing unexpected exits, high-DPI icon rendering, and backdrop rendering on multi-GPU systems.

---

#### Stability & Reliability
- **Resolved Unexpected Exits:** Fixed an issue where the background process could terminate unexpectedly on certain Windows configurations during Start/Search event polling.
- **Optimized Runtime Defaults:** Restored standard .NET garbage collection and heap compaction routines, ensuring consistent stability across prolonged sessions.
- **Diagnostic Logging:** Enhanced diagnostic capture under *Settings › About › Report a problem* to log crash traces safely without recording user data.

---

#### Visual & Display Enhancements
- **High-DPI Icon Rendering:** Replaced fixed 96 px application icons with dynamic 128 px, 192 px, and 256 px asset selection based on monitor DPI scaling (250%+), eliminating blurriness when using large icons or magnification.
- **Transparent Backdrop Option:** Added a dedicated *Transparent* option under *Settings › Appearance › Backdrop*. Shows the clean wallpaper behind the dock without depending on Acrylic or Blur effects that render black on certain multi-GPU laptop drivers.

---

#### Enhancements & Fixes
- Added complete Turkish, German, and Spanish translations for the new backdrop settings.
- Expanded automated test coverage with 12 additional unit tests (370 total).

---

### Download & Installation
- **Installer:** Download `DockHub-Setup-0.9.2-x64.exe` below. Existing installations can also update directly from *Settings › About*.
- **Requirements:** Windows 10 (1809+) or Windows 11 (64-bit).
